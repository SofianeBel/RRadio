Windows Overlay Application

RRadio

RRadio is an open-source Windows desktop application that renders a transparent, non-intrusive radio HUD over your PC games. It emulates 8 authentic radio stations with 139 streaming tracks and integrates Discord Rich Presence.

App Functionality

  • Radio Stations: Stream 8 continuous GTA Vice City style radio stations with seamless static audio effects without requiring any user account.
  • Curated Mixes: Play built-in retro synthwave, 80s pop, and rock mixes instantly.
  • Optional On-Demand YouTube Music: Connect your personal Google account to browse and play your own YouTube Music playlists directly from the in-game overlay.

Google User Data & Transparency

Why does RRadio request access to your Google account?

RRadio requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly sensitive scope exclusively to:

  • Read titles, covers, and track lists of your personal YouTube Music playlists.
  • Display these playlists in the local On-Demand radio queue so you can play them in-game.

Data Privacy & Security:

  • Read-Only: RRadio never modifies, uploads, likes, or deletes any content on your YouTube account.
  • Local Storage Only: OAuth tokens remain on your personal computer. RRadio has no central server, backend database, or tracking service.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your personal data is never sold, shared, or transferred to external third parties.
